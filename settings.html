Settings

Appearance, tab presets and performance. Everything saves locally to this browser, instantly.

Appearance

Theme Dark is default; Light is NULL visually inverted.
Accent A restrained accent; grayscale stays the identity.
Performance mode Disables hover movement and shadows for slower devices.

Browser tab presets

The preset overrides NULL’s tab title and favicon everywhere — including games/apps inside the player. Default is Google Slides–style.

Untitled Slide - Google Slides Default (Google Slides)

Privacy & behavior

NULL is static and local-first — no server ever sees your data.

Cloaking & popups

about:blank and blob: modes are available from the player toolbar. They need a user gesture — the browser controls its own popup prompts. Games stay inside NULL and never open arbitrary destinations.

Danger zone

Whole-NULL resets. Recently played has its own clear button on the home page — this is only for full resets.
