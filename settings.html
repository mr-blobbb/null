Settings

Appearance, tab presets and performance. Everything saves locally to this browser, instantly.

Appearance

Theme Dark is default; Light is NULL visually inverted.
Accent A restrained accent; grayscale stays the identity.
Performance mode Disables hover movement and shadows for slower devices.

Glow border

A slim 5px neon border around the whole screen edge with a soft glow hugging it. The colors drift in a continuous loop, and moving the cursor to an edge lights up just that spot. Off by default.

Comet mode A bright streak travels around the border. Slightly heavier on the GPU.
Custom colors Pick up to two colors for your glow.

Seasonal theme

NULL quietly follows the seasons — fall leaves, winter snow, spring petals, summer light — drifting behind everything. Turn it off if you want the pure look back.

Turn on Fall theme? Leaves drift behind everything · switches automatically with the seasons.

Browser tab presets

The preset overrides NULL’s tab title and favicon everywhere — including games/apps inside the player. Default is Google Slides–style.

Smart Tab Cloak Cycles the tab between school presets on a timer. Pauses while this tab isn’t open — your pick above returns when it’s off.
Custom tab Your own title and favicon — applied everywhere like any other preset.

Press the key anywhere on NULL to jump straight to a boring-looking site. Useful when someone walks up. The browser tab preset still applies, so the tab already looks like work.
